Onshape helper notes

Select any sentence to see the text bar. Hover the table for its handles. The toolbar sits at the bottom.

What it does

The helper reads master sketches and plate geometry, then plans a whole gearbox for you to confirm. It only places library parts, so every bolt and bearing is one you can buy.

Features

Assembly tools

Gears

Gears, pulleys and sprockets come from a layout sketch or a size you pick.

Shortcuts

Every tool in the bar has a key. Press V to select, H to pan, P to draw and R for shapes.

In the assembly

Shift+A opens the auto-assembler, and A opens part search.

Anywhere

Shift+B opens the ReCalc window over whatever you are doing.

Not in this version

Anything beyond one click is left out, and the helper never edits your own parts.
