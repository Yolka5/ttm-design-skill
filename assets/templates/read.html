How a swerve module turns

Two motors, one wheel, and the reason the robot can drive sideways while it spins.

Driving

One motor drives

The drive motor spins the wheel like any other robot. Its speed sets how fast that corner moves.

A drive command is just a speed, the same as on a tank drive.
